## Theory bank (for proposals in the default domain)
- **Signalling theory** (Spence 1973) — disclosure as a credible signal reducing information
  asymmetry; predicts higher-quality disclosure from firms with genuine strategies.
- **Institutional theory** (DiMaggio & Powell 1983) — coercive/normative/mimetic pressures
  explaining heterogeneous responses to identical mandates.
- **Information asymmetry** (Akerlof 1970; Myers & Majluf 1984) — disclosure quality → cost of
  capital / financing outcomes.
- Adjacent, pull as relevant: legitimacy theory, stakeholder theory, agency theory, resource
  dependence, transaction-cost economics.

## Method patterns that fit Nikkhil's toolkit
- **Quant:** firm-level panel with fixed effects; staggered DiD exploiting phased CSRD
  deadlines as a natural experiment (Callaway & Sant'Anna 2021); PSM for issuer vs. non-issuer
  comparison; greenium / cost-of-capital regressions. Data: company reports, EFRAG/CDP,
  Sustainalytics/MSCI, Refinitiv Eikon, Bloomberg, Dealogic.
- **Qual:** purposive multi-case (Yin 2018) across high-emission sectors; semi-structured
  interviews with sustainability officers/CFOs/IR; thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke 2006).
- **Mixed-methods integration** with a synthesised framework + expert-workshop validation
  (the WP1/WP2/WP3 shape of the INN proposal).
